Welcome To Tripod!
Thanks for choosing Tripod to build and host your website! We've made getting started as easy as 1-2-3!

 Already know your way around? Click here and you won't see this page again.


1. Get Ready!
When you joined Tripod, we reserved a Web address for you. We also created a Coming Soon page for your site. But we didn't build your pages. We leave the fun part up to you!

Your Web address is based on your member name (example: http://mysite.tripod.com). To check on your Web address, and get other details about your premium membership, visit the My Account page. To see your Coming Soon page, simply click on your Web address. This is a placeholder page. It will be replaced by your site after you build and publish your pages.

Want to move your website to Tripod? Find out all you need to know here!


2. Build Your Site!
To start building your site, visit the Build page and choose the tool that's right for you!



New to site-building? Give Site Builder a try! You can create a great-looking page in minutes ... without knowing a bit of HTML! When you're ready to update your site, just click on Site Builder to resume building!











If you're comfortable with code, our HTML Editor will let you create pages from scratch. You can also use the HTML editor of your choice (Microsoft FrontPage, etc.)





3. Enhance Your Site!
Take your site to the next level by exploring more of what Tripod has to offer!

To make your site more fun and interactive, visit the Tools page. You can add a customizable poll (and more!) from HTML Gear, get FREE graphics in the Image Gallery, and pick up the latest tips and tutorials from Webmonkey. And when you're ready to profit from your pages, check out Small Business for advice on how to turn clicks into cash. Need inspiration? Visit our favorite Member Sites!

Want to get rid of the ads on your page? Upgrade your site! You'll get MORE room for your files, MORE bandwidth for more visitors, and LIVE customer support. Plus, you'll do it ALL without ads getting in the way! Learn more | Upgrade now!

Did you know you can choose your own Web address? We make is easy to register a domain!
